- **After a pause, focus stays on Speak, so Enter there listens again instead of sending** (whole-branch review fix F1, `f92db6c`; re-review, 2026-11-25). Chosen so that an Enter meant for Stop, just after the browser ends listening by itself, cannot send unread words (the design's first anti-goal). In Chrome a mouse click also focuses the button (MDN `<button>`: "Most browsers do give focus to a button being clicked, but Safari does not"), so a mouse user who pauses and then presses Enter to send restarts listening; nothing is sent and the words stay. Refinement offered: keep focus on Speak only for a keyboard press (`event.detail === 0` on the click, UNVERIFIED), which brings the race back for a mouse user pressing Enter to stop. Next: the owner's call, best after the spoken hand check (README spoken list, steps 2 and 9). Refs: `src/wra/api/static/index.html` `endListening`, `docs/VOICE_PLAN.md` Deviations.
